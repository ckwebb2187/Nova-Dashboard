Lock screen & unlock
Lock dim
On the lock screen the wallpaper stays calm and dimmed, close to your Always On Display, with Samsung's clock on top. When you unlock, it brightens, Nova's clock settles into place and your cards glide in. For the smoothest look: apply Nova to Home and lock screens, turn on "Show lock screen wallpaper" in Always On Display settings, and set Samsung's lock screen clock to a green color in the same spot as Nova's clock.
Cards always make room for Nova's clock, so they never cover it. Glass clock restyles the clock to match the cards, with a shimmer, digits that drop in when they change, and a frosted date pill.
